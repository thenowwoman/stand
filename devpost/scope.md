# Stand

A mobile-first profile and opportunity tool for young creatives in emerging markets to package their skills, set credible prices, and share a professional identity with clients and funders.

## The Unique Kernel
Stand helps people who already know how to do valuable digital work turn that into a client-ready professional identity. Instead of scattered screenshots and vague “I do a bit of design,” it turns their existing skills into a simple profile with service offerings, pricing guidance, and proof-of-work that feels credible to a client.

## Who It's For
A young Nigerian creative, roughly 18–35, who already does useful work online but does not think of it as a business yet. This is the person who runs a church or small-business social media page, edits videos, designs graphics, writes content, or creates digital assets in their spare time. They are on their phone, working informally through WhatsApp, Instagram, and friends. They want to start earning real income without needing a full agency setup or a polished portfolio.

## The Core Loop
The user opens Stand, adds their skills and a few samples, then gets a simple profile that says what they offer, how much it could cost, and how to present it professionally. They can share the profile with a client, save it as a proposal, and discover relevant training or opportunity leads that fit their work.

## Inspiration & Identity
Mobile-first and credible, not corporate. It should feel like a polished WhatsApp/business card hybrid: warm, local, practical, and shareable. The vibe is “professional enough to get paid” without feeling intimidating or fake. It should help someone move from “I do a bit of design” to “Here is my service, here is my rate, here is my proof.”

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner wants to build something that helps skilled people in emerging markets turn existing abilities into income, starting with Nigeria. This is meaningful because many people already have the ability but lack the packaging, pricing, and visibility to get paid fairly.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A user can create a simple, shareable profile for one service type — for example, Instagram design or video editing — with 3–5 sample pieces and a suggested price range based on local market context. The app shows a clean one-page profile and a client-ready message. A reviewer can understand the value immediately: this is a young creative who can offer a service and looks credible enough to hire.

## The POC Boundary
In scope:
- One creative persona and one use case
- A simple profile builder for a single service category
- Suggested service packages or prices in local terms
- A shareable public profile or preview page
- A small opportunity or training discovery section

Out of scope:
- Full marketplace or hiring platform
- Real payments or invoicing
- Full user accounts and multi-tenant setup
- Large-scale grant matching or complex funding flows
- A general social network or full portfolio platform

## Later
- More service categories and richer portfolio management
- Matching with jobs, gigs, and training opportunities
- Grant or program recommendations based on profile data
- Better pricing logic and market intelligence
- Client messaging or project intake flows

## Explicitly Cut
- Building a full freelance marketplace
- Real payment processing
- Broad “all skills” onboarding for every profession
- Complex AI-powered matchmaking before the user and value are proven
- A complete app ecosystem with community features
